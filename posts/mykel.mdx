### about myself
i am 22 years old and i consider myself just another person on the internet. i like programming in java but i also like typescript (and javascript, i guess...), php (mostly because of laravel honestly) and rust.
i also have some knowledge with html, css, c# and python. the list will get longer, i can assure you that but it'll take a liiitle bit..

outside of programming, i like writing which is what pushed me to make a small blog like this. i don't often know what to write about but nevertheless, i like doing so. reading is also something i do but less often
than writing; mostly because i'm busy with other things which doesn't allow me to read as often as i'd actually wish... or maybe i'm just too lazy to find time, both works really. i also play games! minecraft and roblox
are the first on the list but i also enjoy fields of mystria, frostpunk, geometry dash, terraria, webfishing when it was still relevant, melatonin, class of '09 and a lot other visual novels. though, i don't really play
games at the moment. mostly because of college but also because i don't really enjoy playing games recently, hope it'll fix itself soon!

outside of those, i am what people would call a "bus-lover"... or a transit enjoyer? whichever, i really like studying and learning about my local transit agencies, their stations, bus and train routes and all that. i have
a personal website where i can navigate all the public gtfs and gtfs-rt data that they give out! i can't really tell you where this passion came from but i'm really enjoying it so i won't elaborate futher :D

### music taste
i'll be the first one to admit that my music taste is not exactly coherent or logical but i like to think i listen to everything except for country, jazz and classical music. those just bore me to a point. but i do enjoy a
lot of genre, not limited to: shoegaze and post-shoegaze, post-rock, post-metal, hyperpop, metal, extreme metal, heavy and black metal, hip-hop, drill, rap, ambient, noise rock and who knows what people call their genres 
nowadays.
