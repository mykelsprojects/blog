It's no secret that the old version of the blog was bare bone; i thought so too. So, in the last couple days, I've thought of redesigning it. instead of starting from scratch, i've just reused old code and adapted since, you'll get to know, i am a lazy ass bitch :)

## constraints
no database, no cdn, no nothing. the only thing i can realistically use is a github repo for dynamic content;
<GitHubRepo repo="mykelsprojects/blog" description="blog posts and random assets for the blog. #yeah" />

since i am using [vercel](https://vercel.com) as my provider for the time being, i am not willing to pay for a database.. specially since going to school is already quite expensive on the monthly budget...

## inspiration
i've always been fascinated with very fancy, well designed, highly designed website. i think they're neat and i aspire to learn how to design that... maybe one day. in the meanwhile, i like browsing [awwwards](https://awwwards.com). here's a few websites that looks awesome,
* https://www.dontlookup.app/
* https://www.agrumeafarm.it/en
* https://yaas-energy.com/
* https://grail-app.com/

nevertheless, let's get into blogging. i've found interesting websites with interesting designs during my search for inspiration. of course, since im limited, i can't exactly 

here's a few websites i liked;
* https://www.theboringblog.ai/
* https://www.thefriedegg.com/
* https://www.hollywoodexhibit2026.com/

i think each of these have their own charm but what inspired me to revamp my blog is [Evil Martians' chronicles](https://evilmartians.com/chronicles/). I've known their website since i discovered what [oklch](https://oklch.com/#0.7,0.1,347,100) is, thanks to [shadcn-ui](https://ui.shadcn.com/) back in 2023. i love how their website look, especially the post list, where the posts are layered at a different x value every 2 rows. i also like how they display their tags at the top, and how fast they are to sort through posts. i'm trying to copy that directly with my blog, fun fact!

i could go on and on with this but just know that a lot of the decisions i've taken to redesign this was made with their blog in mind. i liked the simplicity of my own blog; being distraction free, fast loading and overall just awesome so i'd rather keep that instead of changing it. 

## future considerations
this isn't the final form of the blog, or the website itself. i'm planning so much things about it, not only to make it more personal to myself, but also to challenge myself to create a digital space where i can fully express, tell and stand for my own opinions. but all that will take time and i have a lot to learn before even starting to tackle all of that so until then, it'll just be a simple blog with silly content and random things about everything and nothing.

until then,
